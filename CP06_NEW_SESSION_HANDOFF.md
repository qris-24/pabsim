## Known limitations (do not overclaim)

This is a **physical card-zone / resolution migration**, NOT the final legal Magic stack implementation. Opponents still cannot cast or respond; fully arbitrary priority windows, counterspells and spell-copy objects, APNAP ordering, and comprehensive targeting/legality remain incomplete. `stack_cards` remains a per-UID ledger, now checked one-to-one against physical cards in `zones['stack']`. Other outstanding audit issues remain tracked in `ISSUE_LEDGER_CP06.json`. S21 and Phase 9 NO-GO. Deck, Savra and Lesson not changed; no optimization or replacement testing.
